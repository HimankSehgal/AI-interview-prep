# Meesho — Data Scientist II 🛍️

## Overview

Meesho has one of the best DS teams in India, being led by Debdoot Mukherjee. They are doing genuinely great work in data science — fraud detection, inventory optimisation, personalisation at scale for Bharat.

This one was one I really wanted.

**Structure:** 3 rounds, straight into the loop (no separate screening)

| Round | Duration | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | 1.5 hours | DSA + SQL + Statistics + Math |
| Round 2 | 1 hour | ML Depth |
| Round 3 | 1 hour | ML Breadth + System Design + Culture Fit |

---

## 💻 Round 1 — DSA + SQL + Statistics + Math

This is the most demanding round. 1.5 hours covering a lot of ground. Do not underestimate it.

You are allowed to Google for syntax and library functions. Solutions are evaluated on time/space complexity, test case clearance, code readability and modularity.

### DSA

Hard level question asked:

| Question | Difficulty | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting Rooms III | Hard | [LC 2402](https://leetcode.com/problems/meeting-rooms-iii/description/) |

💡 I didn't clear this round. DSA practice tha nahi uss waqt and that's on me. If you're prepping for Meesho, start with DSA. This round will catch you if you don't.

### SQL

Two tables given:

**Table 1 — Products**
| product_id | product_name | price |
|---|---|---|

**Table 2 — Orders**
| username | product_name | category |
|---|---|---|

Task: Merge the two tables and find category wise sales.

Concepts tested: JOIN, UNION operations, GROUP BY, aggregations.

### Project Discussion

Discussion on my Vision Transformer project — image similarity search using Siamese Networks and triplet loss.

Questions asked:
- What is a Vision Transformer?
- How does a transformer process an image?
- How do you create patches from images?
- What are image embeddings?
- What is a Siamese Network?
- What is triplet loss? Walk through the math.
- What is a CNN?
- Why use CNN? What are the advantages over a standard ANN?

---

## 🤖 Round 2 — ML Depth

Advanced ML/DL/GenAI theory based on your background and projects. They go deep on whatever you have worked on.

Topics covered:
- Linear Regression — assumptions, loss derivation
- Tree based models — Random Forest, XGBoost, when to use which
- NLP fundamentals
- Graph Neural Networks
- CNN architecture
- LLM fundamentals
- Transformers and Attention mechanism

Know whatever is on your resume cold. They will go deep in your area.

---

## 🔬 Round 3 — ML Breadth + System Design + Culture Fit

This round covers a lot of ground:

**Project Deep Dive**
- Tech stack used
- Model lifecycle — data → training → evaluation → deployment → monitoring
- System design of your solution

**General ML System Design**
- How do you test and deploy ML models?
- How do you monitor model performance in production?
- When do you retrain?

**New Problem Statement**
- They give you a problem outside your experience domain
- Tests how you think and structure your approach, not just what you know

**Culture Fit**
- Your background and motivations
- How you handle ambiguity and ownership

---

## 🎯 Overall Takeaway

Meesho is structured and thorough. The interview process is well thought out and transparent about what to expect.

What to focus on:
- **DSA first** — Round 1 will catch you if you skip this. Practice Hard level problems.
- **SQL** — aggregations, joins, union operations. Don't skip this either.
- **Stats fundamentals** — Bayes, CLT, hypothesis testing
- **Your projects inside out** — Round 2 goes very deep

If your DSA is strong, the rest of the loop is very manageable.
